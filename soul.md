# Soul

## What this game is

A fashion brand builder that lives in the browser. You sit down, you design a real garment, and by the end you have something you'd actually want to exist in the world.

It is not a dress-up game. It is a design tool with game mechanics wrapped around it.

## The feeling

You open it and feel like you just walked into your own design studio. The choices feel meaningful — like picking a material or a silhouette actually matters. The finished piece looks like something. You name your brand. You release the collection. It feels earned.

## The core loop (v1)

1. **Design** — work through four design phases for a single garment: silhouette → material → colour & pattern → details
2. **Brand** — name your brand, choose an identity word, write a one-line brand story
3. **Release** — see your collection card. The piece looks real. Press release.

## What makes it different

The things you design can be manufactured. The game secretly produces a real tech pack — the same document a fashion designer sends to a factory. Players who fall in love with their design can take that document to Canton Fair and source it for real.

The game is the first step of a real supply chain.

## The soul in one sentence

A beautiful, fast creative tool that tricks you into starting a fashion brand.

## What it is not

- Not a simulator of business spreadsheets (yet)
- Not a dress-up doll
- Not an idle clicker
- Not complicated

## Maturity arc

- **v1 (today):** Design one hoodie or t-shirt. Name your brand. Release it. Download your tech pack.
- **v2:** Multiple garments, collections, brand strategy mechanics
- **v3:** Market simulation, trend curves, competitor brands
- **v4:** Real-world integration — supplier connections, sampling, production

## Tone

Confident. Editorial. Like a brand that takes itself seriously but isn't trying too hard.
The UI should feel like a premium design tool, not a Flash game.
