## 1. Garment Taxonomy

The following is the full category tree the app should cover, organised to align with Canton Fair Phase 3 product groupings.

### 1.1 Canton Fair Alignment

Canton Fair Phase 3 (Textiles & Garments) covers:
- Men's & Women's Clothing
- Underwear & Loungewear
- Sports & Casual Wear
- Furs, Leather, Downs & Related Products
- Fashion Accessories & Fittings
- Textile Raw Materials & Fabrics
- Shoes
- Cases & Bags

### 1.2 App Category Tree

```
Tops
  ├── T-shirts (crew, V-neck, polo, ringer, pocket tee)
  ├── Shirts (Oxford, flannel, chambray, dress, camp collar, Hawaiian)
  ├── Blouses (gathered, pussy-bow, wrap, tunic)
  ├── Bodysuits
  ├── Crop Tops
  ├── Tank Tops & Vests (ribbed, spaghetti, muscle)
  └── Sweatshirts & Hoodies

Bottoms
  ├── Trousers (tailored, wide-leg, straight, cigarette)
  ├── Jeans (skinny, straight, wide-leg, bootcut, baggy)
  ├── Shorts (bermuda, cycling, denim cutoff, tailored)
  └── Skirts (mini, midi, maxi, A-line, pencil, wrap, pleated)

Outerwear
  ├── Jackets (denim, leather, harrington, trucker, field)
  ├── Blazers & Suit Jackets
  ├── Bombers
  ├── Coats (trench, overcoat, peacoat, duffle, wrap)
  ├── Puffer / Down Jackets
  └── Parkas & Anoraks

Dresses & Jumpsuits
  ├── Dresses (mini, midi, maxi)
  │   ├── Shift, Sheath, Wrap, A-line, Slip, Shirt Dress, Smock
  │   └── Evening / Occasion
  └── Jumpsuits & Playsuits

Knitwear
  ├── Sweaters / Jumpers (crewneck, V-neck, rollneck/turtleneck)
  ├── Cardigans (open, button-through, cropped)
  └── Knit Tops & Co-ords

Activewear & Swimwear
  ├── Leggings & Tights
  ├── Sports Bras & Crop Tops
  ├── Shorts & Bike Shorts
  ├── Track Jackets & Joggers
  ├── Swimsuits (one-piece, bikini, tankini, rash guard)
  └── Wetsuits

Accessories
  ├── Bags (tote, shoulder, crossbody, backpack, clutch, bucket, belt bag)
  ├── Hats (baseball cap, bucket hat, beanie, wide-brim, trucker)
  └── Belts (leather, canvas, chain, braided)
```

## 6. Knitwear

### 6.1 Gauge (Knitting Machine Needle Density)

| Gauge | Yarn Weight | Typical Products |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| 3–5gg | Ultra-chunky | Statement chunky sweaters, winter outerwear knits |
| 7gg | Chunky / bulky | Heavy winter sweaters, cables |
| 10–12gg | Medium / worsted | Most commercial sweaters, cardigans |
| 14gg | DK / sport | Lightweight sweaters, fine-detail knitwear |
| 16–18gg | Fine / fingering | Luxury cashmere, high-detail fashion knits |

### 6.2 Ply
- 1–2 ply: standard casual crewneck, layering pieces
- 4 ply: heavier cold-weather weight
- 8–16 ply: chunky, shawl cardigans, statement knits

### 6.3 Yarn Fibre
| Fibre | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Lambswool | Soft, warm, affordable; most common mass market |
| Merino wool | Fine fibre; itch-free; mid-premium |
| Cashmere | Premium; 2-ply standard; 18gg typical |
| Cashmere blend (20–30% cashmere) | Cost compromise |
| Cotton | Breathable; lightweight; summer knitwear |
| Cotton-linen blend | Summer; textural |
| Acrylic | Low cost; durable; pill risk |
| Lambswool-acrylic blend | Mid-market standard |
| Mohair (often blended) | Halo effect; brushed finish |
| Alpaca | Warm; hypoallergenic; premium |
| Recycled wool / recycled cashmere | Sustainable option |

### 6.4 Construction Method
- **Fully fashioned**: Each panel knitted to shape; minimal waste; superior fit; quality signal
- **Cut-and-sew**: Panels cut from knitted fabric; faster; standard for mid-market
- **Seamless / whole-garment**: Knitted as one piece on 3D knitting machines (Shima Seiki, Stoll); no seams; premium; reduces waste

### 6.5 Silhouette & Style
- Boxy / relaxed
- Slim / fitted
- Oversized / drop shoulder
- Cropped

### 6.6 Neckline (knitwear)
- Crew neck
- V-neck
- Turtleneck / Roll neck
- Mock neck / Funnel neck
- Boat neck
- Polo collar (ribbed)

### 6.7 Patterns & Textures
| Pattern | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Cable knit | Twisted rope-like columns; classic |
| Rib (1×1, 2×2) | Alternating knit/purl columns; stretchy |
| Fair Isle | Two-colour repeating geometric motifs |
| Intarsia | Colour-blocked panels; cleaner reverse |
| Jacquard | Multi-colour pattern; floats on reverse |
| Waffle | Square textured grid; casual |
| Pointelle | Eyelet lace-like pattern; feminine |
| Fisherman / Aran | Mixed cables and textured stitches |

### 6.8 Finishes

## 9. Cross-Cutting Design Decisions

These decisions apply across multiple garment categories.

### 9.1 Closure Types (Full Reference)

| Closure | Best For | Quality Signal |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| Zip (coil / YKK) | Activewear, outerwear, bags | YKK brand; smooth action; auto-lock slider |
| Zip (metal teeth) | Denim, leather goods, luxury | Heavier; higher cost; premium signal |
| Zip (invisible / concealed) | Dresses, skirts, trousers | Clean finish; quality of tape and tape colour |
| Button (flat, 2-hole or 4-hole) | Shirts, trousers, blouses | Shell / horn vs. plastic; back finish |
| Button (shank) | Coats, jackets, blazers | Quality of shank attachment |
| Tack button (denim) | Jeans, denim jackets | Logo debossing; copper vs. brass |
| Press stud / snap | Bodysuits, babywear, outerwear | Metal vs. plastic; finish; ring size |
| Hook-and-bar | Trousers, skirts, bras | Metal finish; size; stability |
| Hook-and-eye | Dresses, back closures | Covered or bare; sewn placement |
| Hook-and-loop (Velcro) | Activewear, footwear, workwear | Quality of pile; noise level |
| Tie / drawstring | Casual trousers, bags, hoods | Cord material; tip treatment |
| Frog closure | Coats, blouses, occasion wear | Decorative; braiding quality |
| Toggle | Duffle coats, bags | Horn vs. synthetic; cord quality |
| Magnetic snap | Bags, outerwear | Strength of magnet; plating |

## 10. Construction Quality Signals

### 10.1 Stitch Types & Counts

#### Stitch Types
| Stitch | ISO Code | Description | Application |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| Single lockstitch | 301 | Two threads interlocking; standard woven seam | Most woven garments |
| Chain stitch | 401 | Single-thread loop; slight stretch | Denim seams, hems |
| Overedge / serger | 504/514 | Wraps around raw edge; prevents fraying | Jersey seams, edge finishing |
| Flatlock / Flatseam | 602/605 | Flat, wide cover stitch; no bulk or ridge | Activewear, underwear |
| Cover stitch | 406 | Two needle rows top; looper covers reverse | Jersey hems, bindings |
| Blind stitch | 103 | Nearly invisible from right side | Tailored hems |
| Bar tack | Reinforcement stitch at stress points | Pocket corners, belt loop junctions |

#### Stitch Density (Stitches Per Inch)
| Garment Type | Fast Fashion SPI | Quality SPI |
|-------------|-----------------|------------|
| Jersey / T-shirt | 7–9 | 12–14 |
| Woven shirt / trousers | 9–10 | 11–13 |
| Denim | 8–10 | 9–11 |
| Tailored jacket | 10–12 | 14–16 |
| Performance outerwear | 8–10 | 14–16 |

**Quality signal**: Consistent stitch length throughout (variation of ±1 SPI is acceptable; variation of 8 SPI in one section vs. 16 SPI in another indicates poor quality control). Consistent thread tension (no looping on reverse, no bunching).

---

### 10.2 Seam Finishing

| Finish | Description | When Used | Quality Level |
|--------|-------------|-----------|---------------|
| Unfinished / raw | No edge treatment | Lined garments only | Acceptable if fully lined |
| Pinked | Zigzag-scissor cut edge | Light fabrics; budget | Low |
| Serged / overlocked | Overlock stitch wraps edge | Standard mass market | Mid |
| Bound seam (bias tape) | Raw edge enclosed in bias fabric | Unlined jackets; tailoring | High |
| French seam | Fabric edges enclosed within the seam | Delicate unlined | High |
| Flat-felled seam | Folded over and topstitched flat; two visible stitch rows | Denim; workwear | High durability |
| Hong Kong seam | Each edge bound separately in bias | Unlined tailoring | High |
| Pattern matching | Plaids and stripes matched at all seams | Quality wovens | Premium signal |

**Red flags**: Loose threads, uneven seam allowances, seam grinning (gap when fabric pulled), puckering caused by incorrect tension.

### 10.3 Lining & Interlining

| Element | Fast Fashion | Quality Manufacturing |
|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| Lining fabric | Polyester taffeta; lightweight | Cupro, viscose, acetate; breathable |
| Lining fit | Tight; restricts movement | Ease built in; doesn't pull |
| Lining attachment | Machine-stitched with visible allowances | Clean slip-stitched at hem |
| Interlining | Absent or minimal fusible | Horsehair canvas in chest/lapel (tailoring) |
| Interfacing | Fused (glue bond, delaminates with washing) | Sew-in; or high-quality fusible with heat-set adhesive |
| Sleeve lining | Often absent | Present; attached at hem with jump hem |

**Fused vs. canvas interfacing (tailoring signal)**: Pinch the lapel — if it springs back as a unit it is likely fused; if it rolls softly and separately, it is likely canvas-constructed.

## 11. Decoration & Embellishment

### 11.1 Print Types

| Print Method | Process | Best For | Limitations |
|-------------|---------|---------|-------------|
| Screen print (plastisol ink) | Ink pushed through mesh screen per colour | Bold, opaque on darks; durable | Colour limit per job; set-up cost; plastic hand feel |
| Screen print (water-based ink) | Water-based pigment; absorbs into fibre | Soft hand feel; vintage look | Less opaque on darks |
| Discharge print | Chemical removes base dye; replaces with new colour | Ultra-soft vintage look on darks | Only on reactive-dyeable fibres |
| DTG (Direct-to-Garment) | Inkjet directly onto garment | Full colour; low MOQ; photographic detail | Hand feel can be stiff; less durable wash-over-wash |
| Dye sublimation | Dye transferred via heat; penetrates synthetic fibre | Full-colour allover print; no hand feel | Synthetics only (polyester); poor on cotton |
| Allover print (AOP) | Fabric printed before cut-and-sew | Seamless pattern continuation | High MOQ; layout planning required |
| Heat transfer | Pre-printed transfer applied with heat press | Low MOQ; simple graphics | Peeling over time; less breathable |
| Foil print | Metallic foil applied via adhesive | Metallic accents | Cracks with wear; dry-clean care |
| Puff print | Ink swells on curing to create raised 3D effect | Streetwear; texture | Limited detail |
| Resist / tie-dye | Wax or binding prevents dye reaching fabric | Artisanal; organic look | Variable results; high labour |
| Batik | Wax-resist method; traditional heritage | Premium artisanal; slow fashion | Hand-labour intensive |

### 11.2 Embroidery

| Type | Description | Application | Cost Signal |
|------|-------------|-------------|-------------|
| Flat embroidery | Standard machine embroidery; stitched flat to fabric | Logo, crest, text on chest | Standard |
| 3D puff embroidery | Foam placed under embroidery; raises design | Streetwear caps and chest logos | Mid premium |
| Chain stitch | Loop stitches; vintage, textural, slightly raised | Heritage brands; workwear | Hand or vintage machine |
| Appliqué embroidery | Fabric cut-out stitched onto base garment | Varsity jackets; patches; college | Labour-intensive |
| Patch (woven) | Pre-woven motif; sewn or iron-on | Logo; heritage branding | Distinct from embroidery |
| Chenille patch | Yarn-pile appliqué; varsity letter style | Collegiate; streetwear | Premium; labour-intensive |
| Hand embroidery | Artisan needle-and-thread; Kantha, Chikankari, etc. | Luxury; heritage; slow fashion | High labour cost |
| Cross-stitch / needlepoint | Counted stitches on structured fabric | Cottage-core; craft | Niche; hand or machine |

**Thread types**: Rayon (sheen; standard); polyester (durable; less sheen); metallic (decorative; harder to sew); wool (tactile; limited use).

**Stitch density in machine embroidery**: Higher stitch count = more coverage = heavier feel on garment. Pull compensation and underlay stitches are quality signals for logo stability on stretch fabrics.

### 11.3 Washes & Garment Dyeing

#### Garment Wash Reference

| Wash Type | Method | Visual Effect | Eco Profile |
|-----------|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Rinse wash | Water + softener | Minimal fade; soft hand | Low impact |
| Stone wash | Pumice stones | Worn, faded, soft drape | High water use; stone waste |
| Acid wash / Moon wash | Bleach or KMnO₄ + pumice | High-contrast mottled patches | Chemical waste |

| Sand wash | Sand abrasion | Textured; vintage; matte surface | Moderate |
| Laser wash | Laser burns indigo | Precise patterns; no water/chemicals | Most sustainable |
| Garment dye | Post-construction dyeing | Solid color; slight variation | Moderate |
| Pigment dye | Pigment coats fiber surface | Fades beautifully with wear; vintage aged | Moderate |
| Overdye / Tint | Light dye application over existing color | Colour shift; enhanced depth | Low |
| Tie-dye | Binding + dye; manual process | Organic; unique per piece | Variable |

#### Special Denim Finishes
- Whiskering: abrasion marks at hip/thigh crease (manual or laser)

- Topping: application of color on high points for contrast

## 12. Sizing & Fit Systems

### 12.1 Sizing Label Systems

#### Alpha Sizing
- Standard range: XS, S, M, L, XL, XXL (2XL), XXXL (3XL)
- Extended: 4XL, 5XL (plus-size; curve)
- Petite variants: XSP, SP, MP, LP (shorter torso and limb proportions)
- Tall variants: ST, MT, LT, XLT (longer torso and limb)
- Junior sizing: 0, 1, 3, 5, 7 (US odd-number system)

#### Numeric Sizing (Women's, US)
- Misses: 0–20 (even numbers; 0 = approx. 32" bust)
- Plus: 12W–28W (W designation)
- Petite: 0P–20P

#### Numeric Sizing (Men's)
- Chest-based: 36–54 (suit/jacket chest circumference in inches)
- Waist-based: 28–44 for trousers (waist in inches); inseam separate (28–36")
- Shirt collar + sleeve: e.g., 15.5/34 (collar circumference / sleeve length in inches)

#### Dress Sizes (International Conversion Reference)
| US | UK | EU | FR | IT | JP |
|----|----|----|----|----|-----|
| 0  | 4  | 32 | 34 | 36 | 5  |
| 2  | 6  | 34 | 36 | 38 | 7  |
| 4  | 8  | 36 | 38 | 40 | 9  |
| 6  | 10 | 38 | 40 | 42 | 11 |
| 8  | 12 | 40 | 42 | 44 | 13 |
| 10 | 14 | 42 | 44 | 46 | 15 |
| 12 | 16 | 44 | 46 | 48 | 17 |

### 12.2 Key Points of Measure (POM) by Category

#### Tops / Shirts
- Chest (1" below armhole; half circumference)
- Shoulder width (seam to seam)
- Sleeve length (centre back to cuff, or shoulder seam to cuff)
- Body length (HPS — high point shoulder — to hem)
- Waist (at natural waist, half circumference)
- Bottom opening (half circumference at hem)
- Collar/neck opening

#### Bottoms
- Waist (at top of waistband, half circumference)
- Hip (7"–9" below waist, half circumference)
- Rise (front: waistband top to crotch seam; back: waistband top to crotch seam)
- Thigh (1" below crotch, half circumference)
- Knee (full width at knee)
- Leg opening (half circumference at hem)
- Inseam (crotch seam to hem)
- Outseam (waistband top to hem)

#### Dresses
- All top POMs + skirt length (HPS to hem)

#### Jackets / Outerwear
- Chest, shoulder, sleeve, length (all as tops)
- Across back (between armhole seams)
- Neck drop (front and back)
- Back length (collar seam to hem at centre back)

### 12.3 Grading (Size Scaling)

**Grade increment**: The amount added or subtracted per size step at each POM.

#### Typical Women's Grade Increments (per full size)
| Measurement | Grade Increment |
|------------|-----------------|
| Chest circumference | +1" (2.5cm) per size |
| Waist circumference | +1" (2.5cm) per size |
| Hip circumference | +1" (2.5cm) per size |
| Shoulder width | +0.25"–0.375" per size |
| Sleeve length | +0.25"–0.5" per size |
| Body length (top) | +0.25"–0.5" per size |
| Inseam | Often constant; or +0.25" per size |

**Note**: XS–L typically grades at 1" per size for circumference; L–3XL often increases to 1.5"–2" per size ("plus grading").

#### Typical Men's Grade Increments
| Measurement | Grade Increment |
|------------|-----------------|
| Chest (S→M→L→XL) | +2" (5cm) per size |
| Waist (trousers) | +1" (2.5cm) per size |
| Shoulder | +0.25"–0.5" per size |
| Sleeve length | +0.25"–0.5" per size |
| Shirt collar | +0.5" per size |

### 12.4 Fit Philosophy / Target Body

When briefing a manufacturer, brands specify:

- **Base size**: The size the base pattern is developed and fitted on (typically US Women's M / UK 10 / EU 38 or US Men's M / 38 chest)
- **Fit model measurements**: Actual measurements of the person used for fit sessions; the physical reference for adjustments
- **Ease allowance**: The amount added to body measurements to make the garment wearable; determines fit category

| Fit Category | Ease (Chest, Women's) | Ease (Chest, Men's) |
|-------------|----------------------|---------------------|
| Second-skin / stretch | 0" to –1" | 0" (stretch fabric) |
| Slim / fitted | 1"–2" | 1"–2" |
| Regular | 2"–4" | 2"–4" |
| Relaxed | 4"–6" | 4"–6" |
| Oversized | 6"+ | 6"+ |

---

### 12.5 Size Range Decisions

Key questions a brand needs to answer when specifying a run:

1. **Core range**: XS–XL (5 sizes) is standard minimum for most wholesale buyers
2. **Extended range**: XS–3XL (7 sizes) for broader market reach
3. **Inclusive / extended plus**: Up to 5XL or beyond requires additional grade rules and often new base blocks
4. **Regional sizing**: US, UK, EU, and Asian sizing differ significantly — specify which standard the manufacturer should follow
5. **Petite / Tall**: Require separate length grades and sometimes new sleeve and torso proportions; separate base blocks
6. **Children's sizing**: Age-based (0–3m, 3–6m, 6–12m, 12–18m, 18–24m, 2T, 3T, 4T, 4, 5, 6, 6X, 7, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16) or height-based (cm)

A complete specification a manufacturer needs to quote and sample:

| Document | Contents |
|---------|---------|
| Tech pack cover page | Brand, style name/number, season, colourway, fabric specification |
| Flat sketch (CAD) | Front and back; detail callouts for all construction points |

| Construction notes | Seam allowances; stitch type per seam; pressing requirements; label placement |
| Label & packaging | Brand label, care label, hang tag, poly bag, carton specifications |
| Quality standards | AQL level; inspection criteria; defect classification |
